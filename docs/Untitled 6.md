The **Callippic cycle** (or Calippic) is a particular approximate [common multiple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Least_common_multiple "Least common multiple") of the [tropical year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_year "Tropical year"), the [synodic month](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Month#Synodic_month "Month") and the [day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Day "Day"), proposed by [Callippus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callippus "Callippus") in 330 BC. It is a period of 76 years, as an improvement of the 19-year [Metonic cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metonic_cycle "Metonic cycle").

## Description

[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Callippic_cycle&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Description")

A century before Callippus, [Meton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meton "Meton") had described a cycle in which 19 years equals 235 lunations, and judged it to be 6,940 days. This exceeds 235 lunations by almost a third of a day, and 19 tropical years by four tenths of a day. It implicitly gave the solar year a duration of 6940⁄19 = 365 + 5⁄19 = 365 + 1⁄4 + 1⁄76 days = 365 d 6 h 18 min 56 s.
